B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering

For over 16 years, SVIST's ME department in Sonarpur, South Kolkata has delivered outcome-based education across thermal, design, manufacturing, and industrial engineering. Five dedicated technical assistants support a full suite of workshops and labs, and the department's placement roster spans core manufacturing majors, from Triveni Turbine to Apollo Tyres, which hired 11 SVIST mechanical engineers in a single 2024 drive.

Vision, Mission & Program Outcomes

Vision

To envisage an ambience of excellence, inspiring value-based education, research and development in Mechanical Engineering with a commitment to guide students with competency and cutting-edge proficiency to face challenges of application-oriented environment with confidence and practices to societal problems.

Mission

  • M1To impart quality education, training and awareness to students to meet the technological and socio-economical needs of the country.
  • M2To provide industry-oriented laboratory facilities and apply fundamental based implementation.
  • M3To incorporate cosmopolitan outlook among the students, having connection with the industry and R&D organizations for excellence in teaching, research and consultancy services.
  • M4To mould the young dynamic potential minds to emerge as full-fledged future professionals.

Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

  • PEO1Ensure practicing Mechanical Engineers to contribute to society, industry or academia, maintaining sustainability of healthy environment and ethics in the following fields: Thermal Engineering, Mechanical Design, Manufacturing Science and Technology.
  • PEO2Encourage innovation and creativity with foundation in mathematical and fundamental engineering solutions through application of knowledge, skills and analytical ability for engineering solutions using modern tools and machines.
  • PEO3Engage in urge to pursue higher education and life-long learning and demonstrate team work and leadership qualities with effective communication in the multidisciplinary field like finance that support the overall development of the nation and the world.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)

  • PSO1Inspiring students and preparing them for successful professional careers by applying the knowledge of applied science and fundamental Mechanical Engineering core subjects and advanced Mechanical Engineering software.
  • PSO2Ability to co-ordinate and communicate in groups by diversifying their knowledge domain in different engineering disciplinary area.

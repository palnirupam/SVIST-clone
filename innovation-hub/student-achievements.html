Fresh off the press

Featured wins

Hackathon12 September 2026

Team Ignibrix wins 1st prize in the Hardware Model category at Sustainability Hackathon 2026

A Mechanical Engineering team from SVIST, competing as Team Ignibrix with a biofuel-based hardware model, won 1st Prize in the Hardware Model category at the Sustainability Hackathon 2026 — organised by the Office of Sustainability and the Department of Mechanical Engineering, IEM Kolkata, and held at UEM. The team received a cash prize of ₹5,000 and a Certificate of Excellence.

Innovation Summit30–31 July 2026

Team INDROVE wins 2nd prize at NITTTR Kolkata's AI Leadership Summit 2026

At the Artificial Intelligence Leadership Summit (AILS) 2026 – Shaping Intelligent Futures, organised by the National Institute of Technical Teachers' Training and Research (NITTTR), Kolkata under the Ministry of Education, Government of India, our Mechanical Engineering team INDROVE — Sahanowaz Hossain Molla, Kishor Halder, Arijit Naskar, Dipak Kumar Patra, Sandip Bhandari, Mou Rani Hutait and Arpan Dolui, mentored by Dr. Somnath Das and Dr. Nilanjan Roy — secured 2nd prize among 40+ innovative projects presented by institutions from across the country, finishing behind Uluberia Government Polytechnic and ahead of IEM and Jeppiaar University, Chennai.

Research PublicationJuly 2026

Sarnick Chakraborty co-authors and presents research paper at ICAI'26, Las Vegas

Sarnick Chakraborty (Computer Science & Engineering) co-authored and presented the peer-reviewed paper “Vaibhav: Fine-Grained Energy Management of Data Center Servers Using PowerNap” at the 28th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence (ICAI'26), held as part of the 2026 World Congress in Computer Science, Computer Engineering & Applied Computing (CSCE'26) in Las Vegas, USA (20–23 July 2026) — one of the world's largest annual gatherings in computer science, AI and applied computing. Peer-reviewed at about a 19% acceptance rate, the paper will be published by Springer Nature in proceedings indexed in Scopus, DBLP, ACM Digital Library and Web of Science, among others. Guided by mentors Dr. Tathagata Bhattacharya (Auburn University at Montgomery, USA) and Dr. Koushikk Bhattacharyya, Sarnick presented via an officially submitted recorded video and was awarded a Presentation Certificate by the conference organisers.

Pitch CompetitionJuly 2026

Team INDROVE wins 2nd place at Jadavpur University's business pitch competition

At “I'm Out!! (Shark Tank),” a business pitching competition organised by the Institute of Business Management (IBM), Jadavpur University as part of its annual cultural fest, our team — Mou Rani Hutait, Sahanowaz Hossain Molla, Sandip Bhandari and Arpan Dolui — pitched INDROVE, their robotic system for pipeline inspection and blockage removal, before a panel of judges and secured second place.

IdeathonJuly 2026

Top 100 nationally — Think for Bharat 1.0

Mou Rani Hutait, Sahanowaz Hossain Molla, Kishor Halder and Sandip Bhandari secured a place in the Top 100 nationally in Think for Bharat 1.0, a national-level innovation challenge that celebrates bold ideas and empowers the next generation of change makers — out of 2,000+ registrations, organised by Code Capital & Coffee, with the Presentation Round held on 15 July 2026.

Pitch Competition2025–26

Team INDROVE's national streak — a dozen finals and wins beyond Shark Tank

Beyond their Shark Tank and OMTECH Hackathon wins above, the Mechanical Engineering team behind INDROVE — Sahanowaz Hossain Molla, Kishor Halder, Arijit Naskar, Dipak Kumar Patra, Sandip Bhandari, Mou Rani Hutait and Arpan Dolui, mentored by Dr. Somnath Das and Dr. Nilanjan Roy — reached the Grand Finale of Smart India Hackathon 2025 (Hardware) and went on a run of national recognitions: selected for pitching at the Global Entrepreneurship Summit 2026 and She Innovative 2026 (both at IIT Kharagpur) and Women Startup Meet 6.0 (Parul University); Grand Finalists at the Bharat Startup Grand Challenge (Sister Nivedita University), Viswanova 2026 (MIT WPU Pune) and Biznez Plan 2026 (Jadavpur University); winners at Startup Expo @ Dakshh 2026 (HITK) and Neonate 2026 (GKCEM); Top 6 nationally at Startup Showdown 2026 (IIT Patna); Top 15 finalists at Bharat Tech 2026 (SVIET Chandigarh); a Panelist's Choice Award at Pragati 2026 (Jadavpur University); and participants at the 9th ICCPDM 2026 (HITK).

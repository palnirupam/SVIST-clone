The ecosystem behind the numbers

Research & innovation cells at SVIST

Research & Development Cell (R&D)

A dedicated cell that motivates students and faculty to extend and explore new ideas in upcoming technology, periodically.

IPR Cell

Established in 2023 alongside an awareness campaign on intellectual property rights. The institute has received a grant for IPR-related activity from the West Bengal Government, and has since taken shape as an ecosystem covering awareness, filing support and outcomes: 13 Indian patents and 2 international patents granted in the last five years.

Entrepreneurship Development Cell (EDC)

Periodically organises awareness programmes, mentoring sessions and guidance from successful entrepreneurs across fields, and has taken the initiative to build a start-up ecosystem within the campus.

Institute Innovation Cell (IIC)

Established in the 2020-21 academic year under MoE/AICTE guidelines. Runs a One Day HR & Entrepreneurship Conclave (online and offline), participates every year in the National Level Entrepreneurship Camp organised by IIM Kolkata, and organises regular visits to incubation facilities such as the IIM Kolkata Innovation Park and the NASSCOM 10,000 Startups warehouse. Faculty members have completed the foundational and advanced Innovation Ambassador course, and students and faculty take part every year in the Smart India Hackathon.

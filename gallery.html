Flagship Fest Spotlight

INNOVENTION 2k26

Celebrating Innovation, Creativity & Technical Excellence

31 March – 2 April 2026

Swami Vivekananda Institute of Science & Technology proudly hosted INNOVENTION 2k26, its flagship Technical Fest, from 31st March to 2nd April 2026. The event brought together nearly 600 participants from 15 colleges, creating a vibrant platform for innovation, creativity, technical skills and healthy competition.

Featuring 10 exciting events across technology, robotics, coding, innovation, creativity and e-sports, INNOVENTION 2k26 transformed the SVIST campus into a dynamic hub of ideas and youthful energy. With a prize pool of around ₹1 lakh, the fest celebrated outstanding talent and encouraged students to think, create, compete and collaborate.

From an inspiring inaugural ceremony and innovative technical models to intense competitions and memorable moments of celebration, INNOVENTION 2k26 reflected the spirit of experiential learning and student excellence at SVIST.

Life at SVIST

A campus that feels like home

A 1,20,000 sq ft campus at Dakshin Gobindapur, Sonarpur, with hostels, labs, library, sports and a calendar of fests that alumni still come back for.

Facilities

Hostels

Separate hostels for boys and girls near campus: safe, supervised and affordable (optional, monthly fee).

Centralized Library

120-seat reading room with a book-bank facility so first-generation learners don't have to buy every textbook.

AC Classrooms

20 air-conditioned classrooms (60 seats each) with modern teaching aids.

Laboratories

Department labs covering and exceeding the MAKAUT-stipulated curriculum, run with dedicated technical assistants.

400-seat Seminar Hall

Hosts conclaves, guest lectures and department events year-round.

Wi-Fi Campus

Internet-connected systems across departments and library.

Medical & Mentoring

On-campus medical facility, student mentoring and orientation programmes.

Ragging-Free

Zero-tolerance anti-ragging policy with statutory committees ensure a genuinely safe campus.

Cultural fests

Music, arts, photography clubs & Bengal heritage celebrations like Paschim Banga Dibas

Clubs span photography, music, arts and sports, alongside the IEEE student chapter and the Institution's Innovation Council (IIC). From Paschim Banga Dibas seminars to startup challenges, something is always happening on campus.

Research PublicationJuly 2026

Sarnick Chakraborty co-authors and presents research paper at ICAI'26, Las Vegas

Sarnick Chakraborty (Computer Science & Engineering) co-authored and presented the peer-reviewed paper “Vaibhav: Fine-Grained Energy Management of Data Center Servers Using PowerNap” at the 28th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence (ICAI'26), held as part of the 2026 World Congress in Computer Science, Computer Engineering & Applied Computing (CSCE'26) in Las Vegas, USA (20–23 July 2026) — one of the world's largest annual gatherings in computer science, AI and applied computing. Peer-reviewed at about a 19% acceptance rate, the paper will be published by Springer Nature in proceedings indexed in Scopus, DBLP, ACM Digital Library and Web of Science, among others. Guided by mentors Dr. Tathagata Bhattacharya (Auburn University at Montgomery, USA) and Dr. Koushikk Bhattacharyya, Sarnick presented via an officially submitted recorded video and was awarded a Presentation Certificate by the conference organisers.

Industry partnership — Trellisign Technologies

VLSI & IoT Design Labs

SVIST works with Trellisign Technologies Pvt. Ltd. — a Bengaluru-based engineering solutions company and Cadence Channel Partner — as an industry partner for VLSI, IoT and PCB Design, covering curriculum inputs, faculty upskilling, certified training and student internships.

Cadence Channel Partner

VLSI & IC Design Lab

A dedicated ASIC/VLSI design lab built on the Cadence Level-1 software suite — Virtuoso schematic & layout, Genus synthesis, Innovus physical design, Tempus timing signoff, Voltus power analysis, Pegasus physical verification and OrCAD X for PCB design — paired with AMD Artix-7 and Spartan-6 FPGA boards for hands-on RTL-to-GDSII implementation.

Hands-on ASIC design flow

Industry VLSI Training Program

A structured training program covering the complete ASIC design flow — Specification, RTL Design, Design Verification (SystemVerilog, SVA & UVM), Functional Verification, Functional & Code Coverage, Synthesis, STA, DFT, Physical Design, GDS-II, and Tape-out — with hands-on implementation of Counter, FIFO, UART, ALU, Memory, and AMBA protocols (AXI, AHB, APB, AXI4, AXI4-Lite & AXI4-Stream).

4-layer IoT stack

IoT Lab — Trellisign TRU-IoT Platform

An end-to-end IoT lab spanning all four layers of IoT — sensor-connected nodes, gateway, cloud and analytics — with an edge-AI capable gateway, custom sensor & actuator packs, and 40+ ready-to-run applications across Smart Home, Smart Agriculture and Smart Healthcare.

B.Tech Finishing School

Placement prep that starts in Year 1, not Year 4

Six weeks of last-minute placement prep cannot replace four years of building. The Finishing School is a structured roadmap across every stream: the answer to the one question hiring panels always ask: “What have you built?”

01

Year 1 — Explore

Habits that compound: coding fundamentals, digital presence (GitHub, LinkedIn), first mini-projects and NPTEL starts.

02

Year 2 — Build

Ship things, not assignments: stream-specific projects, technical blogging, hackathons, first outreach to industry.

03

Year 3 — Apply

Differentiate: summer internships with a PPO playbook, semester-long capstone, personal brand and presentation skills.

04

Year 4 — Compete

Convert four years into offers: interview training, off-campus strategy, and placement-drive readiness.

NPTEL certification stacks

Five IIT-endorsed certifications mapped for every stream: CSE, ECE, EEE, ME, CE and AI&DS.

Intensive placement cohort

Resume and LinkedIn sessions already delivered; an intensive coached cohort targets IT majors like TCS, Infosys and Wipro.

Seller mindset training

Communication, presentation and pitching: the seventh criterion recruiters filter for, taught explicitly.

Why this matters for you

Hiring panels filter on seven things: real projects, problem-solving proof, internships, communication, self-directed learning, digital presence, and the ability to pitch yourself. Marks alone cover none of them. The Innovation Hub exists to cover all seven: intern inside a campus startup instead of hunting for certificates, build a capstone with real users, and walk into your placement drive with a GitHub profile and a story no other candidate in the room has.

Combined with SVIST's 30+ industry MoUs and the placement cell's recruiter network, the hub closes the loop: learn → build → intern → get placed.

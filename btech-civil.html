B.Tech in Civil Engineering

SVIST's CE department in Sonarpur, South Kolkata prepares students for India's infrastructure boom with a curriculum integrating structural design, geotechnics, surveying, and construction management. Graduates enter government departments (PHE Govt. of West Bengal at ₹4.8 LPA), infrastructure majors, and consultancy, a placement mix few private colleges in the region match.

Vision, Mission & Program Outcomes

Vision

To prepare students to become quality executives with high standards of professionalism, morals, and ethics by leveraging our great resources, infrastructure, and technology in all professional streams. Similarly, we believe in the overall development of the student's personality.

Mission

  • M1To provide a student-centered learning environment, transmit rich knowledge in the theoretical and practical elements of Civil Engineering, and enable students to achieve industry standards.
  • M2To impart quality and real time education to contribute to the field of Civil Engineering.
  • M3To deal with contemporary issues and to cater to the societal needs.

Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

  • PEO1Graduates will exhibit their competence in the fields of Civil Engineering, as well as related engineering fields.
  • PEO2Graduates will contribute to multidisciplinary studies by utilizing modern tools and developing technology.
  • PEO3Graduates will have the foundation needed to find gainful employment in the core and related areas, or they will have the skills needed to get ready for a successful career and work ethically while meeting technical acquaintance requirements.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)

  • PSO1The graduates of this program will be able to meet the needs of public in the design and execution of quality construction work considering the health, safety, cultural, societal and environmental factors.
  • PSO2The graduates will be able to work effectively as an individual or in a team having acquired leadership skills and manage projects in multidisciplinary environments.

From the Head of Department

Welcome to the Department of Civil Engineering. We are committed to developing competent, ethical, and industry-ready engineers through a blend of strong academics, practical training, and technological advancement. The department promotes research, innovation, entrepreneurship, and interdisciplinary collaboration, and provides training for government service examinations to expand career opportunities. Our mission is to prepare civil engineers capable of addressing infrastructure and sustainability challenges while contributing meaningfully to society, the profession, and national development.

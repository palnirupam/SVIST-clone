About the Institute

Education in the light of Swamiji's vision

Swami Vivekananda Institute of Science and Technology (SVIST) is run by a non-profit trust set up to promote high-standard technological and professional education, preparing students not just for professional challenges, but for every challenge life offers.

Who we are

Established in 2008 at Dakshin Gobindapur, Sonarpur, SVIST is a unit of the Swami Vivekananda Group of Institutes (SVGI). The institute is NAAC accredited with Grade B++ (CGPA 2.82 on a 4-point scale, Cycle 2, valid to May 2030), approved by AICTE, and affiliated to MAKAUT (for B.Tech and MBA) and WBSCTE (for Diploma programmes). Course fees follow Government of West Bengal rules: quality engineering education at honest, regulated fees.

In an era of globalization and rapid technological advancement, our effort is to spread quality education throughout society, in the light of Swami Vivekananda's vision, the mission behind our motto, “Driven by his mission”.

Vision

To provide excellent educational opportunities that are responsive to the needs of our students, many from rural backgrounds and vernacular-medium schools, and transform them to meet and surpass the challenges of our nation's growing socioeconomic and technical demands.

Mission

  • 1Be a student-centric institute imbibing experiential learning skills, addressing the technological and professional needs of society.
  • 2Train students as industry-ready professionals.
  • 3Encourage students constantly towards research and higher studies as part of career advancement.
  • 4Promote co-curricular and extracurricular activities for overall personality development.

Principal's Message

India's abundant manpower is one of its greatest strengths, and transforming this potential into a skilled workforce is essential for national development. Inspired by the ideals of Swami Vivekananda, SVIST is committed to nurturing competent engineers, innovative thinkers, and socially responsible professionals. Alongside academic excellence, SVIST emphasizes ethics, leadership, communication, and personality development. Students benefit from year-round training programmes, aptitude and soft-skills development, software training, GATE coaching, doubt-clearing sessions, video lectures, and sports facilities that support holistic growth.

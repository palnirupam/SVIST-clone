A tripartite MoU with Capgemini and Anudip Foundation feeds a dedicated placement pipeline, and the department runs startup challenges and HR conclaves with industry partners.

Vision, Mission & Program Outcomes

Vision

To be a department of excellence, best-in-class in Global Perspective, in emerging areas of business through teaching, learning and research by transforming young graduates into professional managers.

Mission

  • M1To provide a holistic learning environment, imparting knowledge in theoretical and practical aspects of management and empowering them to meet industry standards.
  • M2Establish an industry-academia interface to transform students into industry-ready managers.
  • M3We aim to inculcate a culture of teamwork with a high level of ethics, trust and morality.

Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

  • PEO1To equip MBA graduates with an excellent academic environment to develop high levels of communication skills, leadership skills, creativity, critical thinking, a sense of responsibility, and a teamwork mentality.
  • PEO2To transform MBA students into managers capable of solving complex business problems using analytical skills and handling business uncertainties and crises with a rational approach.
  • PEO3To enable MBA graduates to apply management principles and practices successfully in their careers in the corporate world. Additionally, students should emerge as seasoned professionals and actively engage in corporate social responsibility while valuing lifelong learning.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)

  • PSO1Analyze and solve management problems using knowledge, skills and abilities across various domains through classroom lectures, role plays, simulation exercises, internships, live projects and other activities during the MBA program.
  • PSO2Demonstrate leadership by fostering teamwork and inter-departmental collaboration to achieve a successful career characterized by high levels of trustworthiness, morality and innovativeness.

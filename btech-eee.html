B.Tech in Electrical and Electronics Engineering

The EEE department at SVIST's Sonarpur, South Kolkata campus (est. 2008, 60 seats) covers power generation, transmission and distribution, and modern process control, with a growing emphasis on renewable energy and eco-friendly technology. Half the faculty hold doctorates, the strongest PhD density at SVIST, and graduates move into both public-sector and multinational careers.

Vision, Mission & Program Outcomes

Vision

The Department of Electrical & Electronics Engineering is endeavoured to produce innovative young professionals having superior technical abilities to successfully navigate through the technologically advanced modern world while continuing to become an inspiration to all Indians.

Mission

  • M1To offer students impactful outcome-based teaching methods to constantly improve learning processes driven by quality lectures, curriculum projects, workshops, and seminars on cutting-edge technologies.
  • M2To transform students into technically proficient, goal-oriented individuals with eco-friendly adaptation of technology suitable for regional and global needs to improve human life.
  • M3To empower students for becoming industry-ready professionals acquainted with requisite skills by constant encouragement and guidance.
  • M4To mentor the students in such a way for serving the role of an ethical engineer in shaping the future of the society.

Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)

  • PEO1To equip students with the analytical and problem-solving skills necessary for applying the fundamental engineering and mathematical concepts in the field of emerging technologies.
  • PEO2To inspire students to adopt foundational skills needed for worthwhile employment in their field of study and allied fields.
  • PEO3Creating events suitable for electrical and electronics engineers to develop their personalities and uphold professional ethics in global environments.

Program Specific Outcomes (PSOs)

  • PSO1Students will be able to work as executives in multidisciplinary sectors of national and international repute pertaining to the skills acquired through the domain knowledge to solve complex problems for the design and analysis of industry relevant systems.
  • PSO2Students will be able to be developed with ethical responsibility to perform as an entrepreneur and hone leadership skills to sustain the demands and challenges posed by the present-day society, economics and environment.

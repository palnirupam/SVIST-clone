Admissions at SVIST

A straightforward, transparent admission process with regulated fees, scholarship support and counsellors who actually pick up the phone.

Admission routes

WBJEE / JEE Main

B.Tech — 1st Year

Qualify WBJEE or JEE Main and choose SVIST during centralized counselling. Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics + Mathematics (per WBJEE board norms).

JELET

B.Tech — Lateral Entry (2nd Year)

Diploma holders enter directly into 2nd year through JELET. A 3-year diploma + JELET rank puts you in a 4-year B.Tech with 3 years to go.

CAT / MAT / JEMAT

MBA

Graduates from any discipline; admission per MAKAUT norms (CAT / MAT / JEMAT accepted). Seven specializations to pick at SVIST.

WBSCTE

Diploma (Polytechnic)

After Class 10, via WBSCTE admission process. Diploma in Computer Science & Technology or Mechanical Engineering.

How it works in 5 steps

  1. 1

    Submit the quick enquiry form (30 seconds) or WhatsApp us your rank/marks

  2. 2

    Our counsellor calls you back the same day with seat availability and fee details

  3. 3

    Visit the campus (recommended); tours run every working day, 10 am – 6 pm

  4. 4

    Complete counselling/admission formalities with our admissions desk

  5. 5

    Verify documents, pay the regulated fee, and secure your seat

Documents you'll need

Class 10 & 12 marksheets, rank card (WBJEE/JEE Main/JELET as applicable), school-leaving/migration certificate, Aadhaar, caste certificate (if applicable) and passport photos. Diploma marksheets for lateral entry.

Worried about fees?

SVIST fees follow Govt. of West Bengal regulations (see the fees page) and eligible students can combine institute scholarships with SVMCM, Aikyashree or OASIS, plus bank-loan assistance. Scholarship details →

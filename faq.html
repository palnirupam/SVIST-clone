Frequently asked questions

Straight answers with real numbers on admission, fees, placements, hostel and more.

How can I get admission to SVIST Kolkata?

Through WBJEE or JEE Main counselling for B.Tech 1st year, JELET for lateral entry into 2nd year (diploma holders), MAKAUT norms (CAT/MAT/JEMAT) for MBA, and the WBSCTE process for Diploma courses. You can also enquire directly; our counsellors guide you through counselling choices.

What courses does SVIST offer?

Six B.Tech programmes (CSE, AI & Data Science, ECE, EEE, Mechanical and Civil) plus a 2-year MBA with 7 specializations and WBSCTE diploma courses in Computer Science & Technology and Mechanical Engineering.

Is SVIST NAAC accredited and AICTE approved?

Yes. SVIST is NAAC accredited with Grade B++ (CGPA 2.82 on a 4-point scale, 2nd Cycle, May 2025), valid up to 18 May 2030. It is also approved by AICTE and affiliated to MAKAUT (B.Tech/MBA) and WBSCTE (Diploma), running since 2008 under a non-profit trust.

Where is SVIST located?

Dakshin Gobindapur, Sonarpur, Kolkata – 700145, in South Kolkata (South 24 Parganas), reachable from Garia, Narendrapur, Baruipur and the surrounding belt. Campus visits run every working day, 10 am – 6 pm.

What is the fee structure at SVIST?

Fees follow Government of West Bengal rules, making SVIST one of the most affordable AICTE-approved engineering colleges in Kolkata. Request the current official fee table from the admissions office. Scholarships (SVMCM, Aikyashree, OASIS, institute schemes) can reduce what you actually pay.

What are the placements like at SVIST?

Across the 2024-26 batches, 580+ SVIST students have received 950+ job offers: 235 students / 364 offers in the 2024 batch, 233 students / 388 offers in 2025, and 116+ students / 198+ offers so far in the ongoing 2026 drives. The highest verified package is TCS at ₹9 LPA (2025 batch), with other top offers including Greenply at ₹7.25 LPA and Adani Group at ₹4.08 LPA, and bulk recruiters like Delphi TVS, Apollo Tyres, Kaynes Technology, BMW Industries and Jubilant FoodWorks hiring 10-20 students per drive. These figures come directly from our Training & Placement Cell register — see the full year-wise breakdown on our Placements page.

Does SVIST have hostels?

Yes: separate hostels for boys and girls at a modest optional monthly fee, plus an on-campus medical facility and a strictly ragging-free environment.

What is the intake for each B.Tech branch?

CSE has 180 seats, the largest at SVIST, followed by ECE with 120, and EEE, Mechanical, Civil and AI & Data Science with 60 each (540 B.Tech seats in total). Seats fill through WBJEE counselling rounds, so enquire early.

Should I choose B.Tech CSE or B.Tech AI & Data Science?

Both are 4-year, MAKAUT-affiliated programmes with identical placement access. CSE (est. 2008, 180 seats, 23 faculty) is SVIST's largest, most established department, with a broad software curriculum spanning development, cloud, cybersecurity and data engineering. AI & Data Science (launched 2024, 60 seats) is newer and more tightly focused, built specifically around machine learning, deep learning, big-data tools and MLOps. Choose CSE for the widest range of software career paths; choose AI & Data Science if you already know you want a career centred on AI, ML and data.

Can diploma holders join B.Tech directly?

Yes: through JELET, diploma holders enter directly into the 2nd year of B.Tech (lateral entry), completing the degree in 3 years.

Does SVIST accept SVMCM (Swami Vivekananda) scholarship?

Yes. Eligible SVIST students regularly avail SVMCM, the West Bengal Govt.'s merit-cum-means scholarship worth up to ₹60,000/year, along with Aikyashree, OASIS and institute scholarships. The office assists with applications.

Is SVIST good for placements compared to other colleges near Garia?

SVIST combines Govt.-regulated (lower) fees with a placement cell that has generated 950+ job offers across the 2024-26 batches with recruiters like TCS, Adani, Apollo Tyres and Delphi TVS. Compare total fees against placement outcomes when evaluating colleges in the South Kolkata belt, and visit the campus before deciding.
